How a pact works

One habit. One week. A few friends.

Set it up in a minute. After that, it's one tap a day.

  1. Day 0

    Write the pact

    Choose what to cut back on, how long it lasts, and what the money is for. Invite friends, or go solo.

  2. Every day

    Check in

    Tap once to say you kept it. What you would have spent moves toward your goal.

  3. Last day

    Settle up

    See who made it on the leaderboard. Whoever finishes last takes a silly dare.
